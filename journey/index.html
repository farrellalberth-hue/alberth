01 / THE METHOD

DON'T JUST
memorize.

Memorization can repeat a formula. Understanding can challenge its assumptions. My process moves from intuition to a model — and finally to evidence, falsification, and real-world application.

01 / ASK THE QUESTION

Question.

What is the underlying claim? Identify incentives, hidden assumptions, the relevant variables, and where the explanation could fail.

02 / BUILD THE MODEL

Model.

Translate intuition into diagrams, equations and numerical examples. Understand the parameters before trusting the result.

03 / CONFRONT REALITY

Apply.

Compare predictions with data. Stress-test the model in markets, monetary policy, business decisions or an AI system.

THESIS / 01 — PRICES

Purchasing power is personal.

Average inflation and an individual's basket are not the same statistic. I want to measure food, rent, education and everyday costs rather than accept a number with no methodology.

THESIS / 02 — LIQUIDITY

What does money expansion actually change?

The COVID-era M2 surge drew me into monetary transmission, asset prices, output, demand and inflation. Money supply growth alone is not a complete causal explanation for rising prices.

THESIS / 03 — FIAT

Trust replaced a gold-convertibility promise.

From Bretton Woods to fiat money, I study the institutions and incentives that make a currency credible — and the costs and limitations of alternative monetary regimes.

THESIS / 04 — CREDIT

Banks shape the economy, not just store deposits.

Commercial-bank lending creates deposits. That raises questions about leverage, capital requirements, who receives credit, and who absorbs losses when financial cycles reverse.

THESIS / 05 — NARRATIVES

Power and incentives deserve scrutiny.

I don't assume that governments, banks or critics always tell the whole story. Claims about manipulation, hidden costs and policy success should be evaluated using independent data and competing explanations.

THE CONTINUING INVESTIGATION

From distrust to disciplined inquiry.

These questions pushed me into macroeconomics, financial history, banking, asset pricing, blockchain and AI. This portfolio records what I am investigating — not a final verdict about the world.
