01 / THE METHOD

DON'T JUST
memorize.

Knowing a formula is not the same as understanding its assumptions. I learn by working from intuition to models and then testing their implications.

STEP / 01

Question.

Find the underlying problem. Identify assumptions, variables, and what the model is really claiming.

STEP / 02

Model.

Translate the idea into a diagram, equation, or numerical example and test it step by step.

STEP / 03

Apply.

Connect the model to market behavior, investment choices, business decisions, or a new AI system.
